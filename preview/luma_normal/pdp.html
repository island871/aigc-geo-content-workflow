The LUMA Everyday Linen Shirt is a gender-neutral long-sleeve shirt made from 100% linen in a relaxed fit, priced at USD 59. It is built for shoppers who want one breathable, easy-to-pair layer across work and commute days, travel, and weekends.

Is This Shirt Right for You?

This shirt makes sense if you prefer natural airflow and unrestricted movement over rigid, tailored dress-shirt structure. Its relaxed fit gives non-stretch 100% linen room across the shoulders and sleeves, making it comfortable during transit, long desk hours, and casual weekend plans.

Skip this style if you need a stiff formal collar for black-tie or strict corporate dress codes, or if you want a garment that can be tossed into a high-heat dryer without special care.
