The LUMA Everyday Linen Shirt is a gender-neutral long-sleeve shirt crafted from 100% linen in a relaxed fit. It is built for everyday rotation across work and commute schedules, travel, and weekends.

Is This Shirt Right for You?

Consider this shirt if you want a breathable 100% linen layer with an easy, unrestrictive drape. Because pure linen has minimal natural stretch, the relaxed silhouette leaves comfortable room across the shoulders and arms for transit, desk work, and casual weekend wear.

Look elsewhere if your workplace requires a crisp, formal dress-shirt silhouette or if you need confirmed technical treatments that are not part of a standard 100% linen garment.
